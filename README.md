AntiSosh – Location-Based Social Networking Platform

Connect with people around you, without needing to know their names.

AntiSosh is a privacy-focused social networking website designed to connect people living in the same neighbourhood through location-based discovery. The platform aims to encourage local social interaction, community engagement, and meaningful connections while allowing users to maintain control over their personal information.

Unlike traditional social networking platforms, AntiSosh focuses on geographical proximity, allowing users to discover nearby people through an interactive map interface without displaying their names publicly by default.

Key Features

Location-Based Discovery: Discover people in your neighbourhood through a map-based interface.

Privacy-First Design: Browse nearby profiles without immediately revealing personal details.

User Authentication: Login and registration interface for accessing the platform.

Interactive Map: Google Maps integration for visualizing nearby locations.

Messaging System: A planned feature for communication between users.

Community Events: A planned feature for creating and participating in local events.

Safety Controls: Planned privacy settings, blocking, and reporting options.

Feedback System: A planned rating and feedback mechanism to encourage responsible community interaction.

Responsive UI: A violet-and-white design theme intended to provide a consistent experience across devices.
